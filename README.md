# Digital Current Account Onboarding & KYC Triage (UK Retail Banking)

*Author:* Titilayo Afolabi | ECBA™ | UK Retail Banking Operations & Business Analysis  
*Domain:* UK Retail Banking, Digital Onboarding, KYC/AML, FCA Consumer Duty

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Problem
Transitioning from legacy branch/manual account opening to a Next-Gen digital onboarding process. Currently, *34% of digital current account applicants abandon the journey* during automated identity verification (KYC) and vulnerability screening. Applicants whose documents fail initial OCR checks or who require accessibility/vulnerability support are routed into a silent 5-day manual back-office queue—driving high customer drop-off, repeat inbound telephony calls, and regulatory risk under *FCA Consumer Duty*.

## 2. Project Objectives & Success Metrics (KPIs)
* *Reduce Onboarding Drop-Off:* Lower digital abandonment from *34% to under 18%* within 90 days of launch.
* *Accelerate Time-to-Account:* Enable *85%+ of standard applicants* to open and fund an account in under *10 minutes*.
* *FCA Consumer Duty Compliance:* Provide a dedicated fast-track callback workflow (under *4 hours) for vulnerable customers without weakening **KYC/AML* or *APP fraud* controls.
